## Prompt Ideas for Project Planning

To help you make the most of AI in your project planning phase,
here are a few more prompt ideas:

- For risk assessment:

```toml
Based on our project plan,
can you identify potential risks or challenges we might face during development?
For each risk,
suggest possible mitigation strategies.
```

- For technology selection:

```toml
We're considering using [Technology A] or [Technology B] for [specific functionality].
Can you compare these options in the context of our project,
considering factors like performance,
ease of implementation,
and future scalability?
```

- For estimating project scope:

```toml
Given our project requirements and component breakdown,
can you help me estimate the overall scope of this project?

Please provide a rough estimate of the total development time
and suggest any areas where we might need
to adjust our expectations or seek additional resources.
```
