### Planning Guide: From Idea to Execution

#### Phase 1: Goal Definition (The "Why")

Before planning, you must define the project's purpose.

- **If you have an idea:**
  - **Define Scope:** What is the core problem being solved? What is the "Minimum Viable Product" (MVP)?
  - **Feasibility Check:** Research existing solutions. How will yours differ or improve upon them?
- **If you don't have an idea:**
  - **Explore:** Browse GitHub trending repositories, technology news, or subreddits related to your interests.
  - **Identify Pain Points:** Look for repetitive tasks in your daily routine that could be automated or improved with software.
  - **Ask for Guidance:** Use AI tools to brainstorm based on your technical interests and skill level.

#### Phase 2: Technical Strategy (The "How")

Once the goal is set, map out the technical implementation.

- **Stack Selection:** Choose your languages, frameworks, and databases based on project requirements and your familiarity.
- **Architecture:** Sketch the data flow. Will it be a monolithic application, microservices, or a simple script?
- **Dependencies:** List the external libraries or APIs you will need and verify their stability.

#### Phase 3: Actionable Planning (The "When")

Transform your strategy into a structured project plan.

1. **Breakdown (Tasking):** Decompose the project into small, manageable units of work (e.g., "Set up API structure," "Design authentication UI").
2. **Prioritization:** Identify the most critical features for the MVP. Use a MoSCoW method (Must have, Should have, Could have, Won't have).
3. **Documentation:** Write down the plan in a dedicated format (like a Markdown `PLAN.md` file). This serves as your "source of truth."
4. **Verification Strategy:** Define how you will test each module (Unit tests, integration tests) before you even begin writing feature code.

#### Phase 4: Execution & Review

- **Iterative Development:** Work in small, focused sprints.
- **Continuous Feedback:** Regularly review your `PLAN.md` against the current codebase to ensure you haven't deviated from the goal.
- **Adaptation:** If technical hurdles arise, update the plan to reflect new constraints rather than abandoning the structure.
